📖 CAT-2 Open Book Collaborative Lending

Facing textbook shortages during open-book exams? Students enrolled in the same subject across different timetable slots (e.g. Slot A1 & Slot C1) can co-lend a single textbook. Exam dates are automatically scheduled so both students use the book for their respective exam without clashing!
